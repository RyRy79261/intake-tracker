Pick a structure

Five ways to arrange the same app: a Today gadget, quick entry, launchable apps and windows. Each one is a clickable prototype, built for a 390px phone first and shown at 1280px wide too. Open one, click around, then come back.

  1. 01ward-console.html

    Ward Console404 OS structure, re-dressed clinical

    Layout
    A system bar with a Start menu and tray, Today and Log gadgets on a desktop, and windows that go full-screen on the phone and float at width.
    Look
    Chrome-grey bars, a faint grid wallpaper, block-segment bars with hatched and inverted over-limit states, and mono figures.
    Open Ward Console →
  2. 02bedside-monitor.html

    Bedside MonitorVital channels, tiled split-pane workspace

    Layout
    Stacked parameter channels with a soft-key Log menu. Apps open as tiled panes with splitters, and the vitals shrink to one annunciator strip.
    Look
    An ICU monitor wall: giant condensed numerals, alarm limits in the corner, Mon–Sun trend bars, and over-limit channels inverted.
    Open Bedside Monitor →
  3. 03flight-deck.html

    Flight DeckPFD tapes, MFD pages, soft keys

    Layout
    An always-on display of Today as vertical tapes, apps as pages in a bezel frame, and bottom soft keys that relabel per page. At width there are two side-by-side displays.
    Look
    Glass-cockpit instruments: tick-marked tapes with target bugs, boxed readouts, and lit key bars on a dark bezel.
    Open Flight Deck →
  4. 04stage-manager.html

    StageCentre stage, window rail, Slide Over entry

    Layout
    One focused window on stage, with open windows waiting as live thumbnails in a rail. Quick entry is a panel pulled in from the right edge over anything.
    Look
    Braun/Rams restraint: a typographic ledger, hairline progress rules with target ticks, ink frames and hard offset shadows.
    Open Stage →
  5. 05field-instrument.html

    Field InstrumentHardware faceplate, keypad, rack modules

    Layout
    A fixed faceplate made of a display module (Today), a keypad module (quick entry) and numbered mode keys. Apps load into a bay, and at width into a rack of modules.
    Look
    Teenage Engineering / Nothing hardware: dark glass display, dot-matrix figures, segmented LED bars and engraved keycaps.
    Open Field Instrument →
